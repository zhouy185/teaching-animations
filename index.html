Teaching Animations

Interactive, step-through animations built for lecture. Pick a demo below.

  • 01Split → Apply → Combine
    Watch pandas' groupby().agg() happen one step at a time: rows sort into groups, a target column is highlighted, then count, sum, and mean build the result live.
  • 02LEFT JOIN, Step by Step
    Watch a SQL LEFT JOIN happen one step at a time: matching rows in two tables light up together, an unmatched row is kept with NULLs, then the looked-up columns are attached.
  • 03Join → Group → Sort
    An INNER JOIN followed by GROUP BY, COUNT, AVG, and ORDER BY: the unmatched row is visibly excluded (not NULL-filled), matched rows regroup by borough, per-group stats count up, and a ranked result table appears.
  • 04Same Table, Twice
    One zones lookup table, joined twice under two aliases to resolve a pickup zone and a dropoff zone — no copy needed. Grouping by the (pickup, dropoff) pair and counting builds an origin–destination result, pivoted into a small grid at the end.
